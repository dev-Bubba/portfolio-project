What drives me -

After 5 years in social services working at a county as a case manager for individuals with disabilities, I saw how professionals struggle with software that was not designed to perfom the tasks it performs. I hope to combine my love of creating applications with my professional experience working in social services to create functional and accessible applications that help professionals do their job with greater efficiency than their current tools allow so they can provide better service to the individuals they serve.
